# MIT OpenCourseWare Map

Every course used in this curriculum, what it is for, and what you can skip.
All links were checked in October 2026. OCW content is © MIT, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

**How to use OCW well:** watch the lecture at 1.25–1.5×, then **do the problem sets** with the solutions closed.
Lectures alone will not make you good at this. The problem sets do.
