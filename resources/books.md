# Books

Keep the list short. If you work through the starred books, you will be ahead of most candidates.

## Probability & interview problems
- ★ **Xinfeng Zhou**, *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews* (the "green book"). Work through every problem.
- ★ **Joseph Blitzstein & Jessica Hwang**, *Introduction to Probability* (free PDF from the authors; Harvard Stat 110 videos are also free).
- **Frederick Mosteller**, *Fifty Challenging Problems in Probability* (Dover). Short and classic.
- **Timothy Crack**, *Heard on the Street*.
- **Mark Joshi et al.**, *Quant Job Interview Questions and Answers*.

## Derivatives & stochastic calculus
- ★ **John Hull**, *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives*. The standard reference. Read ch. 1–21.
- ★ **Steven Shreve**, *Stochastic Calculus for Finance I & II*. Vol I (discrete) goes with p04; Vol II is the rigorous continuous-time version.
- **Jim Gatheral**, *The Volatility Surface*. Ch. 1–3 and 5 for p06.
- **Paul Glasserman**, *Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering*. Ch. 1–4 for p05.
- **Sheldon Natenberg**, *Option Volatility and Pricing*. The trader's view; read it for the QT track.
- **Mark Joshi**, *C++ Design Patterns and Derivatives Pricing*. Goes with p05 and p07.

## C++ & systems
- ★ **Scott Meyers**, *Effective Modern C++*.
- **Bjarne Stroustrup**, *A Tour of C++* (3rd ed.). Fast route to modern C++.
- **Anthony Williams**, *C++ Concurrency in Action*. Required for p09.
- **Randal Bryant & David O'Hallaron**, *Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective* (CS:APP). Pairs with 6.172 and 6.004.
- **Ulrich Drepper**, *What Every Programmer Should Know About Memory* (free paper).
- CppCon talks: Carl Cook, *When a Microsecond Is an Eternity*; David Gross, *Trading at light speed*.

## Statistics, ML & research
- ★ **Hastie, Tibshirani & Friedman**, *The Elements of Statistical Learning* (free PDF).
- **James, Witten, Hastie & Tibshirani**, *An Introduction to Statistical Learning (Python ed.)* (free PDF). The gentler option.
- **Marcos López de Prado**, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*. Ch. 3, 4, 7, 11–14 for p14. Read it critically.
- **Ruey Tsay**, *Analysis of Financial Time Series*.

## Markets & microstructure
- ★ **Larry Harris**, *Trading and Exchanges*. The microstructure bible; read it during Phase 3.
- **Álvaro Cartea, Sebastian Jaimungal & José Penalva**, *Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading*. Goes with p15.
- **Michael Lewis**, *Flash Boys*. Light reading; know the critiques of it as well.
- **Matt Levine**, *Money Stuff* (free newsletter). Read it daily.
